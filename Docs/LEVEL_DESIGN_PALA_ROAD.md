# Level Design Specification: "The Pala Road"

---

## 1. Setting & Environmental Architecture
The game takes place across a continuous, interconnected 3D map set in rural Kerala during a heavy monsoon night:

```
[Level Entrance / Mud Road]
          │
          ▼
   [Laterite Walls & Abandoned Bicycles]
          │
    ┌─────┴──────────────────┐
    ▼                        ▼
[Ward Station A]     [The Pala Road (Dense Jungle)]
(Village Path)               │
                             ▼
                    [Ancient Pala Tree & Ward Station B]
                             │
                             ▼
              [Ancestral Tharavadu (House) & Ward Station C]
                 (Veranda, Inner Rooms, Evidence, Closets)
                             │
                             ▼
                 [Southern Sacred Shrine & Safe Zone]
```

---

## 2. Key Landmark Details

### A. Village Entrance
- Wet mud tracks with reflective puddles.
- Weathered Malayalam road signs: *"പാലാ റോഡ് - വഴി അടച്ചിരിക്കുന്നു"* ("Pala Road - Closed").
- Laterite stone walls topped with moss and wild ferns.

### B. The Pala Road
- Overarching bamboo clumps and coconut palms swaying in storm winds.
- Dense volumetric fog illuminated by intermittent lightning flashes.
- Damaged Ward Station A near the road entrance.

### C. The Ancient Pala Tree (Yakshi's Haunt)
- Massive gnarled trunk with sprawling aerial roots and twisted branches.
- Fallen iron ward stakes with severed copper/iron conductive lines.
- Heavy thermal drop and strong electromagnetic deviations.

### D. The Abandoned Ancestral House (Tharavadu)
- Traditional Kerala wooden carved pillars and red terracotta roof tiles.
- Interior rooms containing 3 critical evidence files:
  1. Handwritten field journal of previous researcher.
  2. Disappearance incident report with photographs.
  3. Ancient palm-leaf / copper plate notes on iron boundary conduction.
- Hiding spots: Wooden rice bin (Nellara) and dark corner wardrobes.

### E. The Sacred Shrine (Kavu Sanctuary)
- Stone lamps (Nilavilakku) glowing with steady flame immune to the storm.
- Checkpoint respite and final safe extraction point.

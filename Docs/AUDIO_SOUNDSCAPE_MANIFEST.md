# Audio & Soundscape Manifest: Kerala Monsoon Horror

---

## 1. Soundscape Design Philosophy
In rural Kerala folklore, horror does not begin with loud orchestral stingers; it begins with the unnatural silence of the forest. When an entity like a Yakshi stalks, tropical crickets and nightjars abruptly stop calling. The only sounds remaining are rain falling upon coconut leaves and the subtle silver jingle of traditional dancer anklets (Chilanka).

## 3. Subtitles & Accessibility
All nonverbal and Malayalam audio cues provide synchronized visual closed captions:
- `[Distantly: Anklets chime between the coconut trees]`
- `[Whisper: "Evideya...?" (Where are you...?)]`
- `[AYAS Alert: Voice mimicry recognized - supernatural lure detected]`
- `[Metallic snapping sound from Ward Post B]`
